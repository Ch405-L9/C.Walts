## Evidence boundaries

- ElevenLabs provides platform behavior and settings guidance, not a universal standard for every voice.
- Google provides advertising-platform creative guidance, not a guarantee of campaign performance.
- Descript and Voice123 provide current industry production guidance; pace values are starting points, not laws.
- The supplied positive recordings remain the project's direct target references.
