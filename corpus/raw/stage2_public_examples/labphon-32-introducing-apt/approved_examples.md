## CW-041 — Stacked prepositional qualifiers

**Before**

At the heart of this collection are discussions of the phonological assumptions behind current approaches to prosodic transcription, the choice of discrete units and their granularity, the consequences of adopting a phonetically transparent transcription system, and the challenges of transcribing under-described languages.

**After**

This collection centers on four discussions. First, it examines the phonological assumptions behind current approaches to prosodic transcription. It then considers the choice of discrete units and their granularity, the consequences of adopting a phonetically transparent transcription system, and the challenges of transcribing under-described languages.
