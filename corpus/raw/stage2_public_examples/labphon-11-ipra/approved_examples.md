## CW-040 — Dense nominalization chain

**Before**

The main goal of this paper is to provide a set of arguments for a two-level approach to prosodic annotation, namely broad phonetic and phonological, and to motivate the need for the development of an International Prosodic Alphabet (IPrA).

**After**

This paper has two main aims. It provides arguments for a two-level approach to prosodic annotation: one broad phonetic level and one phonological level. It also motivates the need to develop an International Prosodic Alphabet (IPrA).
